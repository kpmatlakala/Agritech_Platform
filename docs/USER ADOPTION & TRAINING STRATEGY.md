## **1\. Executive Summary**

This User Adoption & Training Strategy defines the approach to driving platform adoption, building user capability, and ensuring sustained engagement across all user segments of the AFAP Digital Agritech Platform.

The strategy recognises that technology alone does not drive adoption—success depends on:

-   Trust built through community-based champions
-   Relevance demonstrated through immediate value (savings, income, information)
-   Simplicity ensured through intuitive design and ongoing support
-   Incentives aligned with user motivations

The approach is built around a train-the-trainer model, leveraging Community-Based Agents (CBAs) as the primary engine for farmer adoption, supported by structured training materials, continuous support, and a feedback loop that drives continuous improvement.

## **2\. Core Adoption Principles**

| Principle | Description | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1\. Farmer-Centric | All training and support starts with farmer needs, not technology features | Farmers adopt tools that solve their problems |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2\. Learn by Doing | Hands-on practice with real transactions, not theory | Adult learners retain 75% through practice vs. 10% through lecture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3\. Train the Trainer | CBAs train farmers; farmers train other farmers | Scalable, sustainable, culturally appropriate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4\. Immediate Value | Show benefit within first interaction (e.g., advisory tip, order saving) | First experience determines ongoing engagement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5\. Continuous Support | On-demand help available via USSD, SMS, and human channels | Reduces frustration and abandonment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6\. Measure & Improve | Track adoption metrics; iterate based on feedback | Data-driven refinement of approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7\. Cultural Relevance | Local languages, visual aids, relatable examples | Builds trust and understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8\. Incentive Alignment | Rewards for adoption (discounts, recognition, access) | Positive reinforcement drives behaviour change |
| --- | --- | --- |

### **5.2 Basic Farmer Training Curriculum (Level 1)**

Module 1: Introduction (15 min)

-   What is AFAP and what does the platform do?
-   Benefits for farmers (cheaper inputs, credit, market access)
-   Overview of services (USSD, SMS, mobile app)

Module 2: Registration (20 min)

-   How to dial \*123# on feature phone
-   Step-by-step registration walkthrough
-   What to do if you have problems

Module 3: Ordering Inputs (20 min)

-   How to browse products via USSD
-   How to place an order
-   How to track order status

Module 4: Advisory & Credit (15 min)

-   How to get crop tips and alerts
-   How to apply for credit
-   Understanding credit score

Module 5: Practice & Questions (10-20 min)

-   Hands-on practice with USSD
-   Q&A session
-   Follow-up plan
